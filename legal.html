These terms govern the subscription to NOGO GAMES, available at nogo-games.com. Subscribing implies full acceptance of them.

2. The service

NOGO GAMES offers mini-games playable directly in the browser, with no installation.

  • Free access — one game, drawn at random each day, is free for everyone, with or without an account.
  • Member access — the subscription unlocks the entire catalogue, at any time, with no ads.

The catalogue changes over time. Games may be added or removed without this constituting a substantial change to the service.

3. Price and payment

The subscription costs €2.99 per month, taxes included. Payment is made by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through our provider Stripe. No card details are ever collected or stored by NOGO GAMES.

4. Duration, renewal and cancellation

The subscription runs for one month and renews automatically each month until cancelled.

You can cancel at any time, in one click, from the "Manage subscription" area of your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period: access remains open until that date, and no further payment is taken. Amounts already paid for the current period are not refunded pro rata.

The subscription provides digital content supplied immediately, as soon as payment is confirmed.

Under article L221-28 13° of the French Consumer Code, at the moment of payment the subscriber ticks a box by which they expressly request immediate performance of the service and acknowledge losing their 14-day right of withdrawal as soon as access to the catalogue is opened.

Without ticking that box, payment cannot be completed and the 14-day right of withdrawal applies under articles L221-18 and following.

This waiver does not prevent a goodwill gesture: if you are unhappy, write to hugo@nogo-games.com and we will look at every refund request in good faith.
